Does sending a request confirm my date?+

No. Your WhatsApp message starts an enquiry. We’ll check availability and confirm the next steps with you; your date is only booked once we confirm it together.

Which package includes a reel or film?+

Tell us which formats you’re interested in. We’ll confirm what’s included in the recommended coverage and discuss any optional extras and pricing before you decide.

Can I ask for Stories or a different social format?+

Yes. Add your preferences to the enquiry and we’ll confirm availability and any extra costs.

When will I receive the finished photos?+

We’ll confirm the delivery timeline for your photos and any video edits before you decide to book.

Where do you provide photography and films?+

We serve Bengaluru (Bangalore), India’s Silicon Valley. Share your event location in your WhatsApp enquiry so we can confirm availability and any travel charges.

Do you offer wedding photography in Bangalore?+

Yes. Send us your wedding date, venue and the moments you’d like captured. We’ll confirm availability, coverage options and pricing for your Bengaluru celebration.
