# Google Nano Banana Prompts — Snehavisuals Website

**For:** Visual placeholders on `https://snehavisuals.com/beta/`  
**Use with:** [`prd-visuals.md`](./prd-visuals.md) and [`color-scheme.md`](./color-scheme.md)  
**Tool:** Google Nano Banana for still images and the optional illustration below

This file contains copy-ready prompts for the planned visual slots. Generate stills/posters only; actual reels, Stories with motion, and highlight films need a separate video workflow. All people and events generated from these prompts are fictional concept art—not Snehavisuals clients, testimonials, or real portfolio proof.

## Before generating

- Copy the prompt for the asset you are creating. Prompts include the relevant Snehavisuals palette guidance, composition, aspect ratio, and exclusions.
- Generate a few candidates. Save the exact prompt, selected output, and any reference image used with the asset ID.
- For SV-10–12 and SV-14–16, generate one approved reference frame first and use it as a visual reference for the other images when the tool supports references. Keep subjects, clothing, location, lighting, and palette consistent.
- Check the candidate in its actual website crop on desktop and mobile before approving it.
- Do not put generated assets forward as real event work. Clearly mark them as generated concept visuals or replace them with permission-cleared real work.

## SV-01 — Homepage hero: first birthday

**Placeholder:** Main hero photograph (`.hero-art__photo`)  
**Size and layout:** Portrait 4:5; environmental event photograph showing the family, cake, and garden-restaurant setting  
**Filename:** `sv-01-hero-birthday.webp`

**Prompt:**

> Create a polished, photorealistic event photograph of a young Indian couple celebrating their toddler’s first birthday at a contemporary garden restaurant in India in 2026. Capture a genuine moment during the celebration: the toddler is delighted by the cake, one parent helps them hold the cake knife, and the other leans close, laughing softly. The child and both parents are the clear emotional focus, engaged with each other rather than aware of the camera.
>
> Make this feel like a professionally covered milestone event, not a family phone snapshot. Photograph from a few steps away at a flattering three-quarter angle, using the restaurant’s elegant setting to frame the family. Include a beautifully set birthday table and a glimpse of the garden terrace, with a few guests softly out of focus in the distance to give the scene a sense of occasion. Use late-afternoon light balanced with subtle professional fill so faces are luminous and clear; preserve fine detail in the cake, clothing, and decor while keeping the background gently soft. Compose with intention and layered depth, like a standout frame from an editorial event gallery.
>
> Modern Indian restaurant design, stylish but believable celebration styling, contemporary clothes, natural Indian skin tones. Use coral `#EF795D` and sage `#B9C5A6` only as restrained decor accents; keep the overall colors lively and true to life. Portrait 4:5, with the toddler, both parents, cake, and enough venue detail visible to communicate the occasion. Full-bleed photograph.
>
> No home interior, picnic blanket, selfie perspective, phones, visible camera or photographer, grandparents, text, lettering, numbers, cake writing, logos, watermark, borders, collage, or social-media interface. Avoid posed lineup, everyone smiling at camera, excessive luxury, and generic stock-photo staging.
