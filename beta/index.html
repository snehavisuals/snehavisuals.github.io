The little questions

Before you
say “let’s do it.”

We’re keeping answers honest while the booking details are being finalized.

Does sending a request confirm my date?+

No. A request is an enquiry, not a confirmed booking. The team will need to check availability and explain how a date is secured once the booking process is finalized.

Which package includes a reel or film?+

Celebration includes one vertical social-media reel. Premium includes a 60-second social reel and a 2–3 minute cinematic highlight film. Other social formats are not included unless confirmed as an add-on.

Can I ask for Stories or a different social format?+

You can tell us what you have in mind. Story sequences, alternate crops and social post formats need to be confirmed separately for availability and price.

When will I receive the finished photos?+

The Classic package currently lists a digital gallery within 7 days. Delivery timeframes for other packages and video edits will be confirmed before bookings open.

Where are you available?+

Our service area is being finalized. We’ll confirm your event location and any travel charges before a booking is accepted.
